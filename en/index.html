Welcome

The last coastal urban green lung of Playa del Carmen

Over time, Playa del Carmen has developed rapidly, becoming a world-class tourism destination. As a citizen-led initiative, PULMÓN VERDE works to conserve the ecosystems of this coastal natural area, located between Hotel Coco Beach and Beach 88.

The Pulmón Verde Collective registers and studies the species that inhabit this mangrove zone — including Clusia flavia (Maya: Chuunup), a peninsula-endemic plant species that is endangered.

In coordination with the Mexican College of Guides in Responsible Tourism, training programs, diplomas, and workshops help build environmental awareness.

Culture & Ancestral Knowledge

Maya Knowledge: Medicine, Ecology, and Cosmology

Elders are guardians of knowledge. Globalization has eroded intergenerational transmission. Pulmón Verde supports recovery without commercializing or exploiting it.

Communal — The original Maya model: circles within circles, not pyramid hierarchies. The organization that gives strength to the collective.
Reciprocity — Genuine exchange between communities and visitors. Goods and services with real mutual benefit.
Holistic — Science, art, ecology, and spirituality as one integrated system of knowledge.
Intergenerational — Passing knowledge from elders to youth as a life project and cultural resilience.
