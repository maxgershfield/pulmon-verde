## Home — welcome and mission

### Welcome to our portal

Over time, Playa del Carmen has developed rapidly and become a world-class tourist destination. As a citizen-led initiative, **PULMÓN VERDE**, together with other organizations, has worked to conserve the ecosystems of this remarkable coastal natural area—located between **Hotel Coco Beach** and **Beach 88**, in the heart of Playa del Carmen.

After the impact of real-estate development, nature has regenerated and recovered its beauty; many species have now made this magical **PULMÓN VERDE** their home.

Today **EL COLECTIVO PULMÓN VERDE** registers and studies the plant and animal species that live in and visit this enigmatic mangrove zone. Through that work they have found that **Pulmón Verde** is home to a **peninsula-endemic plant species**, *Clusia flavia* (in Maya: **Chuunup** or **k’an chunuup**), which is now **endangered**.

In partnership with the **Mexican College of Guides in Responsible Tourism**, they run **training, diploma programs, workshops, and guided tours** to build environmental awareness among visitors and tourists who get to know the project—believing that by working together we can help the planet find its balance.

They invite you to discover **PULMÓN VERDE** in Playa del Carmen: **join and be part of the change.**

## CESTYMA

**CESTYMA** — *Centro de Estudios en Sostenibilidad Turística y Medio Ambiente*  
**English name on site:** Institute of Tourism Sustainability and Environmental Sciences (**ITSAES**)

### Scientific research center

CESTYMA seeks support from Mexico’s **Secretariat of Science, Humanities, Technology, and Innovation** and an **international counterpart**. It will be the **first research center** located in the tourist city of Playa del Carmen and the **first of its kind in Mexico**.

### Tourism sustainability

**Science** plays a leading role in shaping the **legal framework** in all industries, and **tourism is no exception**. **Responsible tourism** begins with **public policy** and succeeds when **industry actors observe and apply** that framework—so **mechanisms are needed** for **oversight** and **promoting compliance**.

### Environment

The **environment defines a destination’s potential** and is **essential for tourism development**. Yet the environment faces **ongoing local and global pressures**, so **sustainable development** is a **research priority**. Studying the **relationship between tourism and the environment** will help **reduce negative impacts** on the environment and **foster responsible tourism**.

### Quoted statement (callout on page)

> “Reflecting on **environmental deterioration at a global level**, it is clear that **potentially we are all part of the problem**, although **not everyone has the potential to be part of the solution**.”
